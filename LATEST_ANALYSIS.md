<h3>AI-powered visualization tools are reshaping the pre-purchase decision</h3>
<p>Meta's new AI room-visualization feature lets shoppers see real branded products rendered in their own spaces, compare options, and click through to buy directly on brand sites. This continues the shift of AI from a back-office efficiency tool to a consumer-facing discovery and conversion layer, blurring the line between social platforms, visualization tech and brand e-commerce. For luxury and fashion retail specifically, this signals growing consumer comfort with AI-mediated "try before you buy" experiences that could extend from home goods into apparel, accessories and lifestyle categories.</p>
<div class='so-what'><span class='so-what-label'>So what for Value Retail</span> As AI visualization tools mature beyond furniture and home décor into fashion, Value Retail should explore in-village AR/AI mirror or app experiences that let guests preview looks or accessories before entering a boutique — reinforcing physical footfall rather than diverting it to pure online checkout, and giving brand partners a reason to co-invest in villages as testing grounds for phygital retail innovation.</div>

<h3>Retailers are prioritising practical, back-end AI over flashy consumer gimmicks</h3>
<p>Coverage of grocery-sector AI investment reflects a broader retail mood: operators are being advised to lean on tech partnerships to solve immediate operational pain points — inventory, forecasting, staffing — while quietly building the infrastructure for future "agentic" AI that can act autonomously across the supply chain. This is a more cautious, ROI-driven narrative than the consumer-AI hype cycle, suggesting many retailers see AI first as a cost and efficiency lever before a customer experience one.</p>
<div class='so-what'><span class='so-what-label'>So what for Value Retail</span> Village-level operations — footfall forecasting, staffing, tenant sales analytics, and back-of-house logistics for boutiques — are a natural near-term AI investment, and partnering with proven tech vendors (rather than building in-house) could let Value Retail modernize operations quickly while reserving capital and creative energy for guest-facing innovation and brand partnerships.</div>
