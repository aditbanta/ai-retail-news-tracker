<h3>Brands are splitting the "growth" and "customer" mandate into two C-suite roles</h3>
<p>On's decision to break its commercial chief role into two distinct seats — Rebecca Cai overseeing global markets and Alice Delahunt joining specifically to own the customer relationship — reflects a broader retail pattern of separating expansion strategy from experience/personalization ownership. As AI-driven CRM, loyalty and personalization tools mature, brands increasingly want a dedicated executive whose sole KPI is customer lifetime value and experience quality, distinct from the wholesale/market-growth agenda. This bifurcation suggests that "customer chief" roles are becoming a standard fixture at high-growth performance and lifestyle brands, mirroring how retailers are restructuring around data and experience rather than just channel expansion.</p>
<div class='so-what'><span class='so-what-label'>So what for Value Retail</span>As anchor and aspirational brands like On formalize customer-experience leadership separate from market growth, Value Retail should expect these newly empowered "customer chiefs" to be more receptive to conversations about curated in-village experience, data-sharing on visitor behaviour, and bespoke activations — an opening to deepen brand partnerships beyond pure square-footage leasing.</div>

<h3>Creator-led, serialized storytelling and cross-brand loyalty is the new Gen Alpha playbook</h3>
<p>Aéropostale's "Intern Diaries" mini-series and its loyalty tie-up with J.C. Penney (both under Catalyst Brands) illustrate how heritage mall/outlet-era brands are re-engaging younger shoppers through entertainment-first, creator-driven content rather than traditional advertising, while also stitching together loyalty ecosystems across sibling brands to maximize retention and cross-shop. This signals that marketing budgets are shifting toward always-on, episodic content formats designed for Gen Alpha attention spans, and that loyalty programs are becoming multi-brand utilities rather than single-brand tools.</p>
<div class='so-what'><span class='so-what-label'>So what for Value Retail</span>Villages should anticipate tenant marketing teams leaning harder into creator content and multi-brand loyalty tie-ins; Value Retail can position its own loyalty/CRM infrastructure and in-village content opportunities (pop-ups, creator meet-ups, family-oriented activations) as a natural extension for brands chasing Gen Alpha and their parents, strengthening tenant mix appeal to younger, family-driven visitor segments.</div>
